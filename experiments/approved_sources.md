[S1] Sleep basics. Most adults need 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Going to bed and waking up at the same times each day, including weekends, helps sleep quality. Sleep scores from wearables combine time asleep, sleep stages and restlessness, and they change from night to night.

[S2] Caffeine and sleep. Caffeine can stay active in the body for 6 hours or more. Coffee, tea, energy drinks or cola in the afternoon or evening can delay falling asleep and reduce deep sleep, which often lowers a wearable's sleep score. Keeping caffeine to the morning for a week is a simple way to see if it is affecting your sleep.

[S3] Resting heart rate. A typical adult resting heart rate is 60 to 100 beats per minute, and people who exercise regularly are often lower. It changes from day to day with sleep, stress, alcohol, illness, dehydration, hot weather and being less active. The trend over one to two weeks says more than a single reading. Contact the care team if it keeps rising for more than two weeks, or if a change comes with dizziness, chest discomfort or breathlessness.

[S4] Mild nausea when starting treatment. Mild nausea is common in the first weeks of treatment and usually improves as the body adjusts. Eating smaller meals, eating slowly, stopping when you feel full, avoiding greasy or very sweet food, and sipping water through the day can help. Contact the care team if nausea is severe, lasts more than a few days, or you cannot keep fluids down.

[S5] Heart rate variability (HRV). HRV is the small variation in time between heartbeats. Higher HRV often means the body is well rested; lower HRV can follow poor sleep, stress, alcohol, illness or hard exercise. HRV differs a lot between people, so compare your trend with your own baseline rather than with other people.

[S6] Medication delivery and temperature. Your medication is shipped with ice packs to keep it cold. If the ice packs have melted or the package feels warm when it arrives, do not use the medication yet. Contact the pharmacy or the care team: they will check whether it is still safe and send a replacement if needed.
